1.Go to the dataset page:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/chethuhn/network-intrusion-dataset.​

2.Log in to your Kaggle account (or create one if needed).​

3.On the dataset page, click the “Download” button (usually on the right side under the dataset title).​

4.Kaggle will download a .zip file (e.g., network-intrusion-dataset.zip) to your Downloads folder.​

5.Extract the zip file (right-click → “Extract All…” on Windows, or use your OS’s unzip tool) and place the extracted CSV files into your project’s data/ folder (or any folder you use in the project).
